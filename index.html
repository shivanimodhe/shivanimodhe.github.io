About

I make sure software works the way people expect, before it reaches them.

I started my career in 2020 as a Test Engineer at ZenQ, building Selenium automation in C#. At QualityAI I worked as an Engineer on test automation with Selenium and TestNG. Since May 2025 I've been a Senior Software Engineer at Hexagon Mining in Hyderabad, testing the Drillhole Manager (DHM) UI.

What I enjoy most is the detective work: tracing a strange bug back to its cause, then turning that lesson into an automated check so it never returns. Next, I want to step into a Test Lead role, where I can shape the test strategy for a whole product and help other testers grow.
